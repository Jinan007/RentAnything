# Packaging an episode for YouTube

Produce, in one reply:
1. **5 title options** (max ~60 characters), curiosity-led but truthful to the episode: the situation + the twist, no spoilers of the final line. Include the series name in 1 or 2 options ("... | Nokoi Recap").
2. **Description**: a 2-line hook (first 150 characters matter most), a 3-line episode teaser, chapters (`python recap.py chapters`), series/next-episode line, then 3 to 5 hashtags. Add a line that the story and visuals are original and AI-assisted.
3. **Tags**: 12 to 15, mix of genre terms ("manhwa recap", "original manhwa", "system", "regression") and episode-specific words. Never other creators' series names.
4. **Thumbnail**: 3 concepts, each = an image prompt (high contrast, one clear character face with strong emotion, one glowing story object, ~3 words of overlay text) plus the overlay text. 16:9 for long videos, 9:16 for Shorts.
5. **Pinned comment** that asks a question about the cliffhanger.
6. **Shorts cut**: suggest which consecutive scenes (45 to 58 s) make the best teaser, ending on the cliffhanger.

Upload notes for the user: YouTube Studio asks whether the video contains altered or synthetic content. The rules change and mostly concern realistic-looking people, places or events, so tell the user to read YouTube's current policy page and answer honestly (an AI narrator voice is the part most likely to matter). Keep a consistent upload schedule, add end screens pointing to the next episode, and make one playlist per series.
